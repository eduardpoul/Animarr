AreaCinematic / gamerCalm / editorial

The functionality (5-slot Continue Watching hero, named pager, dynamic tags, watch-state edges, favorite star, episode cards with disk + watched icons) stays identical. Only the surface treatment is calmer. You can mix-and-match — e.g. keep the Newsreader display font but bring the crimson accent back, or vice versa.
