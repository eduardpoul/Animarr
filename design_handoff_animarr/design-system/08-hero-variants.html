Section 08

Hero pager · 9 variants

Nine ways to navigate the 5-slot "Continue watching" hero. All keep auto-rotation; the difference is how the user takes manual control with a remote, a keyboard, or a mouse. Selectable in Settings → Appearance → Hero navigation.
